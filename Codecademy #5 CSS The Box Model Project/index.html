Platform 9¾ Frames

Step through the platform and into a place where family memories feel a little magical. 9¾ Frames is a cozy gallery built for those in-between moments — the ones that feel ordinary on the surface but hold something enchanted underneath. Just like the hidden platform tucked between what's ordinary and what's wonderful, this is a space where your favorite family snapshots — holidays, everyday laughter, quiet Sunday mornings — get to live somewhere special. No wands required, just the pictures you already love, framed with a little bit of magic.

Where every wall holds a bit of magic, and every face belongs to our story — welcome to the family that needed no sorting hat to know we belong together.
